Where it comes from

One family, one belt of mustard and shisham.

Our hives travel with the bloom across Punjab — mustard in winter, shisham and wild flowers through spring. The honey is drawn cold, strained once through cloth, and jarred the same week.

No blending across farms. No heating to keep it thin. If it crystallises in winter, that is the honey telling you the truth.
